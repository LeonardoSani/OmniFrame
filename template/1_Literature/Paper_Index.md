# Paper index

Every paper in the library, once. Find a citekey here and follow its links: **pdf** is the
paper as published, **md** the converted text the agent reads, **card** the summary.

Grouping: *not chosen yet* — `/lit-cards` proposes options and the researcher picks one.

A paper enters under `## Unsorted` as soon as its PDF exists. Its md link fills in at
conversion, its card link when the card is written; then it moves into its group.
