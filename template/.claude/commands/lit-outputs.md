Write a literature synthesis. Read `1_Literature/CLAUDE.md` first.

What to write, may be empty: $ARGUMENTS

## 1. What

If nothing was asked, ask what the reading should produce and what the document must
achieve. If the request matches an existing file in `1_Literature/Literature_Outputs/`,
refresh that one.

## 2. Write

Write `1_Literature/Literature_Outputs/<name>.md`, with a short descriptive name. Reach the
papers through `1_Literature/Paper_Index.md` and argue from their cards. Shape, length and
headings are yours.

## 3. Tell me

What was written, and any paper still without a card that would have strengthened it.
