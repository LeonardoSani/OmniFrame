# 5_Results

What came back, and what it means. Where the project stands is in
`0_Project/Where_we_are.md`; its history in `0_Project/Journal.md`.

## `Results.md`

What the experiments showed, in whatever shape fits. A refuted hypothesis is a finding:
record it as one, and never bend a result toward the method.

## `Scenario_Tree.md`

A mermaid diagram of how experiments depend on each other, and what can run now. A
convention that helps: a plain edge `E02 --> E03` when E03 just needs E02 done, a labelled
edge `E02 -->|"metric ≥ t"| E04` when E04 only makes sense on that outcome. Mark an
experiment that became pointless as dropped rather than deleting it.
