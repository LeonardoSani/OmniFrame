# Results

Organised by hypothesis, not by run. Every number here cites a record in `Runs/`.

## H1 — <claim>

**Evidence** — which runs, and what they show.

**Verdict** — supported · refuted · inconclusive. A refutation is a finding; record it
as one.

**Open** — what is still unresolved, and what would resolve it.

## Cross-cutting observations

Things the experiments showed that no hypothesis predicted. Often the most valuable
section — and the one that most often gets written into the next project's literature.
