# High level

The idea before it is formal, in plain words. The researcher writes it; the agent helps
only together with them. `/lit-search` reads this file to propose papers, so what is
vague here comes back as vague literature.

Not the same as the question in [Master](../0-Master.md): that is the crisp ask, two or
three lines. This is the wider seed — intuition, scope, the words the field uses.
Formalised, it becomes [Setup & Method](Setup_and_Method.md).

## Topic

One line: the field, and the problem inside it.

## The idea

A paragraph. What you suspect, want to try, or want to understand. Intuition is welcome
here — nowhere else in `2_Idea/` is it.

## In scope, out of scope

What the literature should cover, and what looks close but is not this project. The
second half saves the most search noise.

## Anchor papers

Papers already known to be central, by title or link. Optional — they calibrate the
search, and their references and citations are often the best leads.

## Keywords

The terms the field uses for this, synonyms included. Different communities name the
same thing differently.
